CONTAINERS

Inspection of Containers and Containerized Cargo

Container inspections are carried out to verify the apparent condition and suitability of containers before loading, as well as to investigate container or cargo damage following transportation incidents. The survey provides independent documentation of the container condition and supports the client's commercial and insurance interests.

WHY IS IT REQUIRED?

Independent container inspections help owners, charterers, shippers, receivers and insurers verify that containers are suitable for loading and that cargo has been transported without apparent damage or contamination. In the event of incidents, the survey provides valuable evidence regarding the condition of both the container and its contents.

How:

Prior to loading, the container is visually inspected to verify its cleanliness, dryness and suitability for the intended cargo. Particular attention is given to structural condition, flooring, doors, locking mechanisms, cleanliness and any defects that could affect the cargo during transportation.
Following transportation incidents, the container is inspected for visible damage, deformation, evidence of seawater ingress, water staining and any other conditions that may have affected the cargo.
Where required, the surveyor attends the opening of the container, verifies the integrity of the original line seals, witnesses the removal of the seals and, if necessary, supervises the application of new security seals after completion of the inspection.
When cargo inspection is required, container stripping is attended and the apparent condition of all accessible cargo units is examined and documented. Comprehensive photographs are taken throughout the inspection process. Add:
Container number and seal number verification.
CSC Safety Approval Plate (если инспекция пустого контейнера).
Evidence of repairs.
Condition of roof, side panels and flooring.
Door gaskets and watertightness (визуально).

WHAT IS INCLUDED?

  • Inspection of empty containers for cleanliness and suitability
  • Verification of container readiness for the intended cargo
  • Inspection of damaged containers after transportation incidents
  • Inspection for signs of seawater ingress or contamination
  • Inspection of container doors, seals and locking arrangements
  • Attendance during container unsealing where required
  • Supervision of container stripping and cargo inspection
  • Inspection of cargo condition inside the container
  • Photographic documentation
  • Preparation of detailed survey reports

RESULTS AND REPORTING

Upon completion of the survey, a detailed report is issued containing observations regarding the condition of the container, its contents, photographic documentation and descriptions of any deficiencies identified during the inspection. The report provides independent documentary evidence supporting insurance claims, cargo investigations and commercial settlements.
