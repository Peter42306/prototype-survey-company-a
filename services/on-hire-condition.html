ON-HIRE CONDITION SURVEY

Independent Vessel On-Hire and Off-Hire Condition Inspection

An On / Off Hire Survey is carried out to document the visual condition of a vessel at the time of delivery to the charterers (on-hire) or re-delivery back to the owners (off-hire). The survey establishes the vessel's apparent condition at the transfer of responsibility and provides independent documentary evidence for all contractual parties.

WHY IS IT REQUIRED?

An independent On / Off Hire Survey helps owners and charterers minimise the risk of disputes concerning the vessel's condition at the beginning or end of a charter period. A detailed inspection and photographic record provide reliable evidence of any existing damages, deficiencies or deterioration present at the time of the survey.

How

The survey is performed immediately before the vessel is delivered to the charterers or re-delivered to the owners. All accessible external areas are visually inspected, including cargo spaces, hatch covers, decks, hull structure and mooring arrangements. Each cargo hold or cargo tank is individually examined and photographed. Particular attention is given to fresh structural deformations, dents, coating condition, corrosion, surface rust and any visible defects that may affect the vessel's operational condition. Relevant statutory and operational certificates are reviewed where applicable to confirm their validity in relation to the scope of the inspection.

WHAT IS INCLUDED?

  • Inspection of the vessel's overall external condition
  • Detailed inspection of cargo holds or cargo tanks
  • Visual inspection of hatch covers, rubber packings and compression bars
  • Inspection of the main deck, forecastle deck and poop deck
  • Inspection of the vessel's hull for fresh dents, deformations and damages
  • Inspection of accommodation for general apparent condition
  • Inspection of mooring equipment and deck fittings
  • Assessment of coating condition, corrosion and surface rust
  • Verification of relevant vessel certificates and documentation
  • Comprehensive photographic documentation
  • Preparation of a detailed survey report

RESULTS AND REPORTING

Following completion of the survey, a detailed report is issued containing comprehensive observations, photographic evidence and descriptions of all identified deficiencies. The report provides an independent record of the vessel's condition and may serve as valuable supporting documentation in the event of contractual or technical disputes between owners and charterers.
