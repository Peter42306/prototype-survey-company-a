ON-HIRE BUNKER SURVEY

Independent Determination of Bunker Quantities

A bunker survey is carried out to determine the quantity of fuel oil or diesel oil remaining on board the vessel at the time of inspection (ROB). In case of inspection bunkering operations purpose is to confirm quantity supplied to the vessel from the supplying barge.

On-hire bunker survey is performed on occasion of handing over the vessel from the Owners to the Charterers. One time attendance.

Off-hire bunker survey is performed on occasion of returning the vessel back from the Charterers to the Owners. One time attendance.

Bunkering operations are attended before commencement and upon completion of bunkering operations. Two times attendance. Incudes initial and final measurements of the supplied vessel and the supplying barge.

An independent bunker survey helps owners, charterers and operators accurately establish bunker quantities and minimise the risk of commercial disputes.

WHAT IS INCLUDED?

  • Thorough sounding or ullaging, depending on content level, of all bunker tanks
  • Measurement of fuel / diesel oil temperature in each measured tank
  • Calculation of bunker volumes using approved vessels trim tank calibration tables
  • Calculation of volume of content in each tank
  • Applying temperature corrections for each tank
  • If the vessel bunkered after time of deilvery, bunkers quantities at the time and place of delivery are properly recalculated
  • Calculation of quantities remaining on board for each declared grade
  • Recalculation of bunker quantities to the time and place of vessel delivery or re-delivery, what applicable
  • During measurement special attention is paid for possible blind pipes, inaccurate tank gauge readings and other procedures during measurements that can lead to mistakes
  • Preparation of bunker survey reports and certificates

RESULTS AND REPORTING

Bunker Survey Certificate is issued together with detailed calculation sheets showing the quantities of fuel remaining on board.

Certificate of Delivery is prepared in case of on-hire survey inspection, showing details of time and place of delivery, along with quantities of bunkers recalculated for the date and time of delivery.

Certificate of Re-Delivery is prepared in case of off-hire survey inspection, showing details of time and place of expacted redelivery, along with quantities of bunkers recalculated for the expected date and time of re-delivery.

Bunker Survey Certicicates are issued for the supplied vessel and for the supplying barge with details of measuremetns, temperatures. gauges, and calculations.
