STEEL PRE-SHIPMENT INSPECTIONS

Independent Inspection of Steel Products Before Shipment

Steel Pre-Loading Inspection is carried out to verify the apparent condition of steel products before loading on board the vessel. The survey provides independent documentation of cargo condition, identifies existing damages and supports the proper issuance of cargo documents and survey remarks.

WHY IS IT REQUIRED?

A cargo tank inspection confirms that the cargo tanks are suitable for loading the intended cargo and helps minimise the risk of contamination, cargo quality deterioration and loading delays. Independent certification provides confidence to owners, charterers, shippers and receivers that the cargo spaces comply with the required loading standard.

How

The inspection is performed at storage facilities before loading begins. Each cargo consignment is visually examined for dents, deformation, corrosion, damaged edges, packaging defects and any other apparent deficiencies. Particular attention is given to cargo identification marks and segregation of individual shipments.
Where required, observations are prepared in accordance with the cargo documentation, allowing survey remarks to be incorporated into Mate's Receipts, Bills of Lading or other agreed shipping documents.
During loading operations, the surveyor attends cargo handling to supervise stowage and loading procedures. Any new damage occurring during loading is immediately documented and reported to the relevant parties.
Photographs are taken throughout the inspection, including general views of the storage area, individual cargo consignments and all identified damages.

WHAT IS INCLUDED?

  • Inspection of steel products at storage areas before loading
  • Inspection of hot rolled, cold rolled and coated steel products
  • Inspection of steel coils, billets, slabs, rebars and structural sections
  • Verification of cargo consignments and identification marks
  • Documentation of existing damages and deficiencies
  • Preparation of cargo remarks by shipment or cargo consignment
  • Supervision of loading operations
  • Monitoring of stowage and cargo handling
  • Recording of any damage occurring during loading
  • Comprehensive photographic documentation
  • Preparation of detailed inspection reports

RESULTS AND REPORTING

Upon completion of the inspection, a detailed Cargo Tanks Inspection Certificate is issued together with a comprehensive photographic report. The documentation records the condition of each cargo tank, observations made during the inspection and the surveyor's assessment of the vessel's suitability for loading the intended liquid cargo.
