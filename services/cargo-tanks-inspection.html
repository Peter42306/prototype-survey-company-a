CARGO TANK INSPECTION

Inspection of Cargo Tanks for Cleanliness and Suitability to Load Intended Cargo

A Cargo Tank Inspection is carried out before loading to verify that cargo tanks are clean, dry and suitable for loading the intended liquid cargo. The inspection is performed for edible oils in accordance with applicable requirements.

WHEN REQUIRED

An independent Cargo Tank Inspection confirms that the cargo tanks are suitable for loading the intended cargo and helps minimise the risk of contamination and loading delays. Independent inspection provides confidence to owners, charterers, shippers and receivers that the cargo tanks comply with the applicable tank cleanliness standard.

WHAT IS INCLUDED?

  • Verification that cargo tanks are clean and dry
  • Verification of suitability to load the intended liquid cargo
  • Inspection for residues of previous cargoes
  • Inspection for foreign odours and contamination
  • Inspection for water remaining on tank steelwork and in drain wells
  • Inspection for signs of leakage from heating coils
  • Inspection of coating condition in coated tanks
  • Inspection of stainless steel surfaces in stainless steel tanks
  • Verification of previous cargoes compatibility where required
  • Verification of cargo tank cleaning procedures
  • Review of cargo tank cleaning methods, water temperatures during cleaning and cleaning chemicals used
  • Photographic documentation of each cargo tank
  • Preparation of inspection reports and certificates

RESULTS AND REPORTING

A Cargo Tank Inspection Certificate is issued together with a detailed photographic report. The documentation records the condition of each cargo tank, observations made during the inspection and the surveyor's assessment of the vessel's suitability for loading the intended liquid cargo.
