STOCK INSPECTIONS

Independent Inspection of Cargo at Storage Facilities

Stock Inspection is carried out to verify the presence, apparent condition and declared quantities of cargo stored at warehouses, terminals or open storage facilities. The survey provides independent confirmation of the cargo status before shipment, sale or further transportation.

WHY IS IT REQUIRED?

An independent stock inspection helps owners, traders, insurers and financial institutions verify that the declared cargo is physically present, properly stored and in an acceptable apparent condition. The survey provides reliable documentary evidence supporting commercial transactions and inventory verification.

How

The survey is performed at warehouses, terminals or open storage areas where the cargo is stored. The surveyor verifies the apparent condition of the cargo, assesses the storage arrangements and confirms that the cargo is stored in a manner appropriate for its intended use.
Where required, the declared quantities are verified against the available cargo documentation and observations are made regarding the condition of the storage area, accessibility of the cargo and any visible deficiencies affecting the cargo.
Photographs are taken throughout the inspection, including general views of the storage facility and detailed photographs of the cargo and any observed deficiencies.

WHAT IS INCLUDED?

  • Verification of cargo presence at the storage facility
  • Inspection of the apparent condition of the cargo
  • Verification of declared quantities where applicable
  • Inspection of storage conditions
  • Inspection of packaging and cargo identification where required
  • Comprehensive photographic documentation
  • Preparation of detailed inspection reports

RESULTS AND REPORTING

Upon completion of the inspection, a detailed Cargo Tanks Inspection Certificate is issued together with a comprehensive photographic report. The documentation records the condition of each cargo tank, observations made during the inspection and the surveyor's assessment of the vessel's suitability for loading the intended liquid cargo.
