PETROLEUM PRODUCTS INSPECTION IN CONSTANTA

Independent Inspection of Petroleum Product Cargo Operations

UNIS INSPECTIONS provides independent petroleum products inspections in Constanta, Romania, for owners and charterers during vessel loading and discharge operations. We assess cargo tank readiness, measure ullages and calculate cargo quantities within the agreed inspection scope.

Our attendance and reporting give clients a documented record of cargo quantities and observed transfer operations. These findings support quantity reconciliation and help clients address discrepancies during loading or discharge.

SERVICE INCLUDES

LOADING

  • Inspection of empty vessel cargo tanks for readiness to load cargo
  • Attendance during loading operations
  • Ullage measurements upon completion of loading
  • Cargo quantity calculations
  • Sealing of cargo tanks
  • Vessel's bunker survey before and after loading
  • Comprehensive photographic documentation where required
  • Preparation of survey report

DISCHARGE

  • Unsealing of cargo tanks
  • Ullage measurements before commencement of discharge
  • Cargo quantity calculations
  • Attendance during discharge operations
  • Inspection of empty vessel cargo tanks to verify emptiness after completion of discharge
  • Vessel's bunker survey before and after discharging
  • Comprehensive photographic documentation where required
  • Preparation of survey report

REPORTING

A detailed Final Survey Report is issued together with cargo tank inspection records, ullage measurements, cargo quantity calculations and comprehensive photographic documentation where required. The report records inspection findings and other observations relevant to the agreed inspection scope.
