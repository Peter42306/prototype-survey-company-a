CARGO HOLD & TANK CLEANING

UNIS INSPECTIONS arranges cargo hold and cargo tank cleaning services in Romania, Bulgaria and other ports by request. Cleaning works can be carried out after discharge to remove previous cargo residues, dust and contamination and to prepare cargo spaces for the next intended cargo.

Where required, we can also attend cargo hold & tank cleanliness inspections and assist with preparation for possible re-inspection and final acceptance.

SERVICES

  • Cargo hold cleaning and washing
  • Cargo tank cleaning and washing
  • Removal of previous cargo residues
  • Removal of loose residues, dust and contamination
  • Cleaning after coal, ore and other bulk cargoes
  • Preparation of cargo holds and tanks for the next intended cargo
  • Cleaning before cargo hold or cargo tank inspection
  • Attendance at cleanliness inspections
