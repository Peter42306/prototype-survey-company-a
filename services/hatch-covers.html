HATCH COVERS INSPECTION

Inspection of Cargo Tanks for Cleanliness and Suitability

Cargo Tanks Inspection is carried out before loading to verify that cargo tanks are clean, dry and suitable for the intended liquid cargo. The inspection is commonly performed for edible oils and other food-grade liquid cargoes in accordance with the applicable industry requirements and charter party instructions.

WHY IS IT REQUIRED?

A cargo tank inspection confirms that the cargo tanks are suitable for loading the intended cargo and helps minimise the risk of contamination, cargo quality deterioration and loading delays. Independent certification provides confidence to owners, charterers, shippers and receivers that the cargo spaces comply with the required loading standard.

How

Each cargo tank is visually inspected from the tank entrance to verify its cleanliness, dryness and suitability for the intended cargo. Particular attention is given to residues of previous cargoes, foreign odours, remaining water, contamination and any condition that could affect cargo quality.
For coated tanks, the condition of the tank coating is examined together with any signs of damage, contamination or coating defects. For stainless steel tanks, the stainless steel surfaces are inspected for cleanliness, product residues and general condition.
The surveyor also reviews the cargo tank cleaning procedure, including the use of cold water, hot water and approved cleaning chemicals where applicable. Particular attention is paid to confirming that the cleaning process complies with the requirements for the intended cargo.
Heating coils are visually checked for signs of leakage, and tank drain wells are inspected to ensure that no free water remains after the cleaning process.
Each cargo tank is individually inspected and photographed to provide a complete record of its condition before loading.

WHAT IS INCLUDED?

  • Inspection of cargo tanks for cleanliness and dryness
  • Verification of suitability for the intended liquid cargo
  • Inspection for residues of previous cargoes
  • Inspection for foreign odours and contamination
  • Inspection for water remaining on tank surfaces and in drain wells
  • Inspection for signs of leakage from heating coils
  • Inspection of coating condition in coated tanks
  • Inspection of stainless steel surfaces in stainless steel tanks
  • Verification of cargo tank cleaning procedures
  • Review of cleaning methods, temperatures and cleaning chemicals used
  • Photographic documentation of each cargo tank
  • Preparation of inspection reports and certificates

RESULTS AND REPORTING

Upon completion of the inspection, a detailed Cargo Tanks Inspection Certificate is issued together with a comprehensive photographic report. The documentation records the condition of each cargo tank, observations made during the inspection and the surveyor's assessment of the vessel's suitability for loading the intended liquid cargo.
