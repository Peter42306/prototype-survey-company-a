HATCH COVER INSPECTION

Visual Inspection of Vessel's Weather Deck Openings

A Hatch Cover Inspection is carried out to estimate the general condition of the vessel's hatch covers and weather deck openings. Rubber seals, compression bars, quick-acting cleats and other hatch cover components are inspected, and all visible deficiencies together with recommendations for corrective action are documented in the survey report.

WHEN REQUIRED

A Hatch Cover Inspection is carried out before loading moisture-sensitive cargoes, as part of vessel condition inspections, or as a periodic inspection requested by owners, insurers or P&I Clubs.

SERVICE INCLUDES

  • Inspection of hatch cover panels, tops and undersides
  • Inspection of rubber seals and compression bars
  • Inspection of quick-acting cleats and locking devices
  • Inspection of hydraulic cylinders, operating chains and other operating mechanisms
  • Inspection of hatch coaming drain channels and drain valves
  • Inspection for presence of traces of sealing tape or expanding foam on the hatch cover joints
  • Inspection for signs of leakage and water ingress
  • Inspection of ventilation openings, covers and operating mechanisms
  • Inspection of access manholes
  • Photographic documentation
  • Preparation of inspection reports

REPORTING

A detailed survey report is prepared containing observations, photographic evidence and descriptions of all identified deficiencies and recommendations. The report provides an independent record of the vessel's condition and may serve as supporting documentation.
