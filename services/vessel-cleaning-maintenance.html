VESSEL CLEANING & MAINTENANCE

UNIS INSPECTIONS arranges vessel cleaning and maintenance services in Romania, Bulgaria and other ports by request. Works may include cleaning, rust removal, surface preparation, touch-up and general painting of decks, cargo holds, cargo tanks, ballast tanks and other accessible vessel areas.

Similar cleaning and maintenance works can also be arranged for lifeboats, yachts and boats.

SERVICES

  • Cargo Hold Maintenance
  • Cargo Tank Maintenance
  • Ballast Tank Maintenance
  • Deck Maintenance
  • Rust and scale removal
  • Surface preparation
  • Touch-up and general painting works
  • Cleaning and maintenance of lifeboats, yachts and boats
