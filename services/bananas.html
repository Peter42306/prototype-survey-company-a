BANANAS

Inspection of Frozen and Perishable Cargoes

Perishable Cargo Inspection is carried out to assess the apparent condition of temperature-sensitive cargoes such as bananas, fresh fruit, fish, meat and other refrigerated or frozen products. The survey provides independent documentation of cargo condition during transportation and upon delivery.

WHY IS IT REQUIRED?

Independent inspection of perishable cargoes helps owners, insurers, shippers and receivers verify that temperature-sensitive products have been transported under appropriate conditions. The survey assists in identifying any apparent deterioration, temperature deviations or transportation-related damage that may affect cargo quality.

How:

The inspection begins with a visual examination of the refrigerated container following discharge from the vessel. Particular attention is given to any visible damage to the container, evidence of improper handling and conditions that may have affected the cargo during transportation.
Where required, the surveyor attends the opening of the container and supervises container stripping. Cargo temperatures are measured using appropriate temperature probes, including pulp temperature measurements for bananas where applicable.
Bananas and other fresh produce may be subjected to visual examination and cut tests to assess their apparent ripening condition and identify any signs of deterioration or transportation damage. For frozen cargoes, the apparent condition and product temperatures are verified both during container unloading and at cold storage facilities where required.

WHAT IS INCLUDED?

  • Inspection of refrigerated containers after discharge
  • Inspection of container condition and any transportation damage
  • Verification of cargo temperature
  • Inspection of refrigerated and frozen cargoes
  • Inspection of bananas, fruits, fish, meat and other temperature-sensitive cargoes
  • Banana pulp temperature measurements where required
  • Visual inspection and cut tests for bananas
  • Inspection of cargo condition during container stripping
  • Inspection of cargo condition at cold storage facilities
  • Comprehensive photographic documentation
  • Preparation of detailed survey reports
  • Report from ther spot with brief details of alleged damage to the cargo

RESULTS AND REPORTING

Upon completion of the survey, a detailed report is issued containing observations regarding the condition of the container, its contents, photographic documentation and descriptions of any deficiencies identified during the inspection. The report provides independent documentary evidence supporting insurance claims, cargo investigations and commercial settlements.
