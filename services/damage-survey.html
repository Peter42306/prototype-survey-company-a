DAMAGE SURVEY

Independent Investigation of Maritime Damages and Accidents

A Damage Survey is carried out following accidents or incidents involving vessels, cargo, equipment or shore facilities. The purpose of the survey is to independently establish the apparent extent of the damage, document the circumstances of the incident and provide reliable evidence supporting the client's interests.

WHY IS IT REQUIRED?

Independent damage surveys help owners, charterers, insurers, P&I Clubs and other interested parties establish the apparent cause and extent of damage following an incident. Thorough documentation and evidence collection provide valuable support during insurance claims, contractual disputes and subsequent technical investigations.

How:

The survey begins with a thorough visual inspection of the damaged property and the surrounding area. Particular attention is given to identifying the location, extent and apparent nature of the damage, together with any conditions that may have contributed to the incident.
The surveyor collects available documentation, reviews relevant records and, where appropriate, interviews personnel who witnessed the occurrence in order to establish the sequence of events. Statements and observations are carefully compared with the available evidence.
Where necessary, sketches, manoeuvring diagrams and explanatory drawings are prepared to illustrate the circumstances of the incident. Comprehensive photographs are taken of all damaged areas, the accident location and any relevant evidence.
Where appropriate, recommendations may also be made regarding the preservation of evidence until further investigation or repairs are undertaken. Add:
measurement of damages (длина, ширина, глубина деформаций);
identification of affected structural members;
review of log books and VDR data (если предоставлены);
preservation of evidence before repairs;
recommendations for temporary repairs, если это входит в задание.
Measurement of damaged areas where required
Identification of affected structural members
Review of relevant log books and available records
The report is prepared in an objective and independent manner, documenting factual observations without prejudice to the interests of any party.

WHAT IS INCLUDED?

  • Inspection of damaged vessels, cargo and equipment
  • Inspection of damaged port or shore facilities
  • Assessment of the apparent extent of damage
  • Collection of documentary evidence
  • Photographic documentation of damaged areas
  • Review of available ship's documents and records
  • Interviews with personnel involved in the incident
  • Collection of witness statements where applicable
  • Preparation of sketches and manoeuvring diagrams where required
  • Preparation of detailed survey reports

RESULTS AND REPORTING

Upon completion of the survey, a comprehensive report is issued containing detailed descriptions of the damage, photographic evidence, witness information, collected documentation and the surveyor's observations regarding the apparent circumstances of the incident. The report provides independent documentary evidence supporting insurance claims, technical investigations and contractual proceedings.
