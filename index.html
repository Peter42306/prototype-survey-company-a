ABOUT US

UNIS Marine & Cargo Inspections provides independent marine and cargo survey services in Constanta, Romania, and nearby Black Sea ports for shipowners, charterers, traders, P&I clubs, insurers and cargo interests.

Our services include draught surveys, bunker surveys, condition surveys, damage surveys, cargo inspections, sealing and unsealing of cargo holds and containers, tally counts and other marine and cargo surveys.

We focus on accurate reporting, prompt response and professional support throughout every stage of the inspection process.
