ABOUT US

UNIS Marine & Cargo Inspections provides independent survey and inspection services for shipowners, charterers, traders, P&I clubs, insurers and cargo interests.

Our surveyors have extensive practical experience in marine cargo operations, including bulk cargoes, steel products, liquid cargoes and general cargoes.

We focus on accurate reporting, fast response and professional support during loading, discharging and transportation operations.

Based in Constanta, Romania.
